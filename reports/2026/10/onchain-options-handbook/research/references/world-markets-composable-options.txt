                     The Economics of Composable Options Markets
                                 Replication, liquidity formation, and capital efficiency
                                            in composable financial markets

                                                 World Markets Research
                                                        October 2026

                                                         Abstract

        Onchain options are primarily constrained by liquidity. It is possible to construct options using existing onchain
     spot and loan markets. October 2026 market data suggests a 6-12x increase in top of book liquidity is possible for
     onchain calls and puts, relative to Derive V2, by constructing options from this existing liquidity. This opportunity
     would allow traders to exploit market inefficiencies in current onchain options markets. We explore how the unique
     properties of fully onchain matching and settlement unlock a more efficient market structure intermediated by
     dealers in traditional equities and FX options markets.
