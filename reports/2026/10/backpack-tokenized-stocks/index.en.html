Key findings
  1. The volume is structural, not issuance-driven. Backpack's float is 3% of xStocks', yet it out-traded xStocks in single-name stocks from June to August ($2.7B vs $1.45B, Blockworks). Our data: $2.06B over 90 days, 92% of it the stock traded against dollars.
  2. The anchor is instant two-way conversion. Withdrawing a share from Backpack Exchange mints the token on Solana in seconds; depositing burns it back into a brokerage entitlement. $303M moved this way in 90 days, almost all on weekdays.
  3. Conversion lets market makers quote from day one. Because they can dump or refill inventory through Backpack, prop AMMs quoted every listing from launch: 53% of SPCX volume on day one, 62% of Backpack volume in Jul–Aug, versus 86% passive pools for xStocks (Blockworks).
  4. Arbitrage bots then pull every other venue to that price. 33% of volume is atomic arbitrage, mostly prop AMM ↔ public pool. Volume spikes 6.5× in the hour of the US open: the onchain price follows Wall Street.
  5. The result is measurably better execution. Backpack SPCX fills beat xStocks SPCXx by 1.2–2.4 bps against the stock's own quote, and quoted lower round-trip costs in 98% of matched tests. Through Jupiter, even xStocks buyers route 81% via Backpack SPCX.
  6. The weak spots are weekends, size and memes. Conversion nearly stops on weekends, prop AMM depth thins out by $50K, and the September memecoin wave (up to $7.9M a day) faded to under $1M a day by October.
核心结论
  1. 成交来自结构，不是来自发行量。 Backpack 的流通量只有 xStocks 的 3%，但 6–8 月单只股票的 DEX 成交反而超过 xStocks（$2.7B 对 $1.45B，Blockworks）。我们的数据：90 天 $2.06B，其中 92% 是用美元买卖股票本身。
  2. 锚是秒级的双向申赎。 从 Backpack 交易所把股票提到 Solana 就是铸造代币，几秒到账；充回交易所就是销毁、换回券商账户里的股票。90 天走了 3.03 亿美元，几乎全在工作日。
  3. 能申赎，做市商才敢从第一天报价。 库存多了可以换回股票，少了可以从股票换出代币，所以 prop AMM 每只新股一上线就报价：SPCX 首日 53%、7–8 月 Backpack 62% 的成交在 prop AMM；而 xStocks 86% 在被动池子（Blockworks）。
  4. 套利机器人把其他场所都拉到这个价格。 33% 的成交是原子套利，主要是 prop AMM ↔ 公共池。美股开盘那一小时成交暴增 6.5 倍：链上价格跟着美股走。
  5. 结果是可测量的更好成交。 以股票自身报价为基准，Backpack SPCX 比 xStocks SPCXx 便宜 1.2–2.4 bps；配对报价测试里 98% 更便宜。通过 Jupiter，连 xStocks 的买单都有 81% 先经过 Backpack SPCX。
  6. 短板在周末、大单和 meme。 周末申赎几乎停摆；单笔到 $5 万时 prop AMM 的深度明显变薄；九月的 meme 浪潮（最高每天 $7.9M）到十月已不足每天 $1M。

04Why the price holds价格为什么锚得住

Six pieces of evidence, from our onchain data and Blockworks' execution study.

六组证据，来自我们的链上数据和 Blockworks 的成交质量研究。

4.1 Prop AMMs quote from day one, around the clock

4.1 prop AMM 从第一天、全天候报价

Prop AMMs quote from live stock-market data and their own view of fair value, but inventory limits how much they can show. Backpack's two-way conversion removes that limit: excess tokens can be turned into stock, and stock into tokens when inventory runs low. Blockworks finds prop AMMs took 53% of SPCX volume on day one and 64% in its first full week, versus a few percent for xStocks. From Jun 12 to Aug 30 they cleared 60% during market hours, 65% overnight and 59% on weekends. Over Jul–Aug: 62% of Backpack volume on prop AMMs; 86% of xStocks volume in passive pools.

prop AMM 根据实时股价和自己对公允价值的判断报价，但能报多少受库存限制。Backpack 的双向申赎解决了这个问题：代币多了可以换回股票，少了可以用股票换出代币。Blockworks 发现，prop AMM 在 SPCX 上线首日拿到 53% 的成交、首个完整周 64%，而 xStocks 同期只有几个百分点。6 月 12 日至 8 月 30 日，prop AMM 在 美股交易时段占 60%、夜盘 65%、周末 59%。7–8 月 Backpack 62% 的成交在 prop AMM，xStocks 86% 在被动池子。

Our Dune data agrees for the same months (58% prop AMM in Jul–Aug; Dune misses a few prop AMMs). The share fell to about a third from September, not because prop AMMs left, but because memecoin flow arrived on public pools.

我们的 Dune 数据在同一时期得到一致的结果（7–8 月 prop AMM 占 58%，Dune 少解析了几家 prop AMM）。9 月起占比降到三分之一左右，不是因为 prop AMM 退出，而是 meme 流量进了公共池子。

  • Weekdays only: $4.6M a weekday (16% of that day's DEX volume) vs $0.24M a weekend day (2.6%). On weekdays Backpack can route to US exchanges, so arbitrageurs can buy the share and mint, or buy the token and redeem.

4.3 A third of volume is atomic arbitrage

4.3 三分之一的成交是原子套利

We rebuilt every transaction touching a Backpack stock from all its DEX legs. A transaction where every token sold is bought back (e.g. USDC → SPCX on Meteora → USDC on ZeroFi) is a cycle: atomic arbitrage.

我们把每一笔碰到 Backpack 股票的交易，用它所有的 DEX 腿还原出来。如果卖出的每种代币最后都被买回（例如 USDC → 在 Meteora 买 SPCX → 在 ZeroFi 卖回 USDC），就是一个闭环，也就是原子套利。

4.6 Composability turns competing issuers into shared liquidity

4.6 可组合性让竞争的发行方共享流动性

Because SPCX and SPCXx trade near 1:1 in a shared pool, Jupiter routes 81% of $1K SPCXx buys and 64% of sells through Backpack SPCX, letting xStocks users tap Backpack's tighter prop AMM quotes. The reverse is rare (2–5% of Backpack buys, no sells). This matches our finding that cycles against xStocks are only 0.4%: the wrappers are linked by routing, not by arbitrage. Issuers compete on redemption and rights while sharing execution liquidity.

因为 SPCX 和 SPCXx 在一个公共池子里以接近 1:1 交易，Jupiter 把 $1,000 SPCXx 买单的 81%、卖单的 64% 先路由经过 Backpack SPCX，让 xStocks 用户也用上 Backpack 更紧的 prop AMM 报价。反向很少（Backpack 买单只有 2–5%，卖单为 0）。这和我们“对 xStocks 的闭环只有 0.4%”的发现一致：两种代币是通过路由连起来的，不是通过套利。发行方在申赎和权益上竞争，但共享成交流动性。

Where it breaks

短板

  • Weekends. Conversion falls to 1/20 of a weekday and volume drops by two-thirds; prices rest on MM inventory, perps and oracle feeds.
  • Size. The quote advantage narrows from ~30 bps to 1.6 bps at $50K as prop AMM quotes thin out. Pools remain the backstop for larger orders.
  • Breadth. No ETFs. xStocks regained the volume lead in late August with SPY and CRCL.
  • Memes are transient. They doubled weekend activity in September and were mostly gone by mid-October.
  • Concentration. Three names are 65% of volume, and one coordinator (Sunrise) organizes the market makers.
  • 周末。 申赎降到工作日的 1/20，成交少三分之二；价格靠做市商库存、永续和预言机喂价撑住。
  • 大单。 单笔到 $5 万时报价优势从约 30 bps 缩到 1.6 bps，prop AMM 报价变薄，大单仍要靠池子兜底。
  • 品种。 没有 ETF；xStocks 靠 SPY、CRCL 在 8 月底拿回成交第一。
  • meme 不持久。 九月让周末成交翻了几倍，十月中基本退潮。
  • 集中。 三只股票占 65% 的成交；做市商由一家（Sunrise）协调。

08Recommendations for the mStocks ecosystem对 mStocks 生态的建议

Anchor before demand. P0 items should be live at launch; without them, demand will not stick.

先锚后需求。P0 应在上线时就位；没有它们，带来的需求留不住。

R1

Make Bybit ⇄ Mantle conversion the anchor

把 Bybit ⇄ Mantle 申赎做成锚

P0
What
For the launch names, a Bybit withdrawal to Mantle mints mStocks and a deposit burns it back into the Bybit position: two-way, seconds, near-zero cost, open to market makers and arbitrageurs, not only retail.
Evidence
Backpack: $303M converted in 90 days; mint and burn nearly equal each weekday; conversion is what lets prop AMMs quote from day one (Blockworks).
Owner
Bybit + issuer partner; Mantle BD
做什么
上线的股票里，从 Bybit 提币到 Mantle 就是铸造 mStocks，充回 Bybit 就是销毁并恢复 Bybit 上的持仓：双向、秒级、接近零成本，并且对做市商和套利者开放，不只是散户。
依据
Backpack 90 天申赎 $303M；每个工作日铸造和销毁几乎相等；正是申赎让 prop AMM 能从第一天报价（Blockworks）。
负责方
Bybit + 发行合作方；Mantle BD
R2

Coordinate market makers on prop AMMs from day one

从第一天起协调做市商在 prop AMM 报价

P0
What
Play Sunrise's role: line up 2–3 desks per listing that quote mStocks on prop AMM / RFQ venues (Fluxion, plus the dev team's PropAMM + JIT Router work), hedge on Bybit, and rebalance inventory via R1. Keep standing pools as the backstop for larger orders.
Evidence
Prop AMMs: 53% of SPCX volume on day one, 60–65% in every session; Backpack beats xStocks in 98% of matched quotes. xStocks' passive-pool model loses on price.
Owner
Mantle dev team (PropAMM, JIT Router) + BD (MM programme)
做什么
扮演 Sunrise 的角色：每只上线的股票安排 2–3 家做市商，在 prop AMM / RFQ 场所报价（Fluxion，以及 dev team 的 PropAMM + JIT Router），在 Bybit 对冲，通过 R1 调库存；同时保留公共池子给大单兜底。
依据
prop AMM 拿到 SPCX 首日 53% 的成交，各时段都在 60–65%；配对报价里 98% 比 xStocks 便宜。xStocks 的被动池子模式在价格上输了。
负责方
Mantle dev team（PropAMM、JIT Router）+ BD（做市商计划）
R3

Make arbitrage and routing cheap and composable

让套利和路由便宜、可组合

P0
What
Make every mStocks venue routable by the aggregators already on Mantle (OKX DEX, KyberSwap, LI.FI); cheap multi-leg transactions; 1:1 pools linking mStocks with other wrappers of the same stock (e.g. xStocks on Mantle) so routers can share liquidity; publish venue and oracle data so outside bots can run. Goal: external bots replace our own arb.
Evidence
33% of Backpack volume is atomic arbitrage by ~4,000 bots; Jupiter routes 81% of SPCXx buys via Backpack SPCX. On Mantle, our own bot is 36% of xStocks volume.
Owner
Mantle dev team + ecosystem DEX/aggregator partners
做什么
让 Mantle 上已有的聚合器（OKX DEX、KyberSwap、LI.FI）能路由到所有 mStocks 场所；便宜的多腿交易；用 1:1 池子把 mStocks 和同一只股票的其他代币（比如 Mantle 上的 xStocks）连起来，让路由共享流动性；公开场所和预言机数据，方便外部机器人接入。目标是让外部机器人取代我们自己的套利。
依据
Backpack 33% 的成交是约 4,000 个机器人的原子套利；Jupiter 把 81% 的 SPCXx 买单路由经过 Backpack SPCX。Mantle 上我们自己的机器人占 xStocks 成交的 36%。
负责方
Mantle dev team + 生态 DEX / 聚合器
R4

Keep fees Solana-level and sequencing neutral

手续费保持 Solana 水平，排序保持中立

P1
What
Target sub-cent, predictable fees for swaps and multi-leg arbitrage, and publish clear, neutral ordering rules (Flashblocks, priority) so market makers and bots can model execution.
Evidence
Blockworks' critique of Robinhood Chain: fees rose 18× to a $0.075 median (128× Solana) and ordering is operator-controlled. “If every trade… carries meaningful gas costs, sophisticated onchain finance remains uneconomic.” Mantle is an L2 and will be judged the same way; its single sequencer with no public mempool is an anti-sandwich advantage worth stating explicitly to MMs.
Owner
Mantle dev team
做什么
兑换和多腿套利的手续费做到低于 1 美分且可预测；公开清晰、中立的排序规则（Flashblocks、优先级），让做市商和机器人能对执行建模。
依据
Blockworks 对 Robinhood Chain 的批评：手续费涨了 18 倍，中位 $0.075（Solana 的 128 倍），排序由运营方控制。“如果每笔交易都有明显的 gas 成本，复杂的链上金融就不经济。” Mantle 是 L2，会被用同样的标准衡量；单 sequencer、没有公开 mempool，不会被夹，这一点应该明确告诉做市商。
负责方
Mantle dev team
R5

Close Backpack's weekend gap with Bybit's 24/7 market

用 Bybit 的 7×24 市场补上 Backpack 的周末短板

P1
What
Keep R1 conversion open on weekends; give MMs a 24/7 hedge (Bybit stock perps or the planned Mantle Perps); use a robust weekend reference price. Make “best weekend execution for tokenized stocks” a headline.
Evidence
Backpack's weekend conversion is 1/20 of a weekday and weekend volume is a third; its prop AMMs still quote weekends off 24/7 venues and Pyth.
Owner
Bybit + Perps product + oracle partner
做什么
R1 的申赎在周末也开放；给做市商一个 7×24 的对冲腿（Bybit 股票永续或计划中的 Mantle Perps）；周末用可靠的参考价。把“周末成交最好的代币化股票”当成卖点。
依据
Backpack 周末申赎只有工作日的 1/20，周末成交只有三分之一；它的 prop AMM 周末仍参考 7×24 场所和 Pyth 报价。
负责方
Bybit + Perps 产品 + 预言机合作方
R6

上线时品种要少，并借势热点事件

Evidence
Backpack took 45% of Solana tokenized-equity volume on SPCX's IPO day; SPCX, MU, SKHY are 65% of its volume. Mantle has 1,500+ xStocks tickers, 14 above $10K float.

做什么

借贷 / Perps 合作方

09Open questions to confirm待确认问题

  1. Conversion path. Can Bybit and the mStocks issuer support two-way, seconds-level mint/redeem for MMs and users, including on weekends?
  2. Legal claim. What do mStocks holders own, and can they redeem into a brokerage or Bybit position like Backpack's trust receipt?
  3. Market makers. Which desks will quote on Mantle prop AMMs at launch, and what inventory and hedging terms do they need?
  4. Fees and ordering. What is Mantle's median swap fee today, and what can MMs rely on for ordering and latency?
  5. Wrapper linkage. Will mStocks coexist with xStocks on Mantle, and who seeds the 1:1 linking pools?
  1. 申赎路径。 Bybit 和 mStocks 发行方能否为做市商和用户提供双向、秒级、包括周末的铸造/赎回？
  2. 法律权益。 mStocks 持有者拥有的是什么？能否像 Backpack 的信托凭证那样赎回成券商或 Bybit 持仓？
  3. 做市商。 上线时哪些做市商会在 Mantle 的 prop AMM 报价？他们需要什么库存和对冲条件？
  4. 手续费与排序。 Mantle 目前的兑换手续费中位数是多少？做市商在排序和延迟上能依赖什么？
  5. 代币之间的连接。 mStocks 会不会和 Mantle 上的 xStocks 并存？谁来提供 1:1 连接池的初始流动性？
